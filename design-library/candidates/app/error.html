When something is wrong

Each of these replaces the page that could not be shown. It says what happened, what is safe, and what to do next.

Not running

ACP has stopped on this computer

The app is only a window onto ACP, which runs on your computer and owns the agents, worktrees and memory. That part is not answering at 127.0.0.1:4317.

Your work is safe: every task saved a checkpoint as it went, and each run's worktree is still on disk. Working tasks stopped where they were and will show Resume.

or in a terminal:
acp /ui

Likely cause
The computer slept, or the terminal that started ACP was closed
Port in use?
If something else took port 4317, ACP says so when it starts. Change the port in Settings.
